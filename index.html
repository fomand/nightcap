Common questions

How do I play?

The robot introduces new drinks and games as you progress. Pair Match asks you to match identical drinks; Sequence tests the order you remember; No Repeats asks you to choose a drink you haven't selected before; Look Behind asks you to compare a drink with one shown earlier; and Combo Rush challenges you to arrange drinks to complete the combinations on the wall.

How do I change the sound or language?

Open the menu in the corner of the screen. Music and sound effects have separate switches, and the language arrows let you choose your preferred language.

How does level progress work?

Complete the level's games to see your results. Your overall result is the average of your game scores. Reach the level's goal to continue; otherwise, choose Play Again to retry.

What happens when I close the app?

Your level progress is saved on your device. If you close the app during a level attempt, you return to a Start button and restart that level rather than resume a partially completed game.

The app isn't working as expected. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app, then check the App Store for an update. If the problem continues, contact support with the details above. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting, as this may remove progress saved on your device.
